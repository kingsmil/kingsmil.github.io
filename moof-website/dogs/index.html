Why "dirty"

The name's a bit of a joke. The care isn't.

Every dog that walks in has had a good time getting messy first — that's the whole point of being a dog. Our job is the second half of the story: the visual health check, the detangling, the patient work on a matted coat that a rushed groomer would just shave off.

We don't cage, we don't rush, and we don't do ten dogs an hour. If your dog is anxious, we slow down. If your dog is difficult, we've probably met worse. Photo and video updates go out mid-session, so you're never left wondering what's happening behind the door.
