Claim sales

Where claims post

Channel (each card gets its own comment thread): paste the channel as @name or t.me/name. Turn on comments first (channel settings → Discussion → link a group), and make the bot an admin of both the channel (Post, Edit, Delete messages) and the linked group.

Topic: paste a topic link (t.me/c/…/…). The bot must be an admin with Manage Topics and Delete Messages, and you (or the shop owner) must be an admin of the group.

Rehearsals: cards marked [test] post to their own topics, never to the ones above. Each must be a topic link (t.me/c/…/…) in a group you and the bot administer. With the test Claims topic unset, releasing a test claim is refused rather than sent to the buyers’ topic.

Claims start as listings. Pick cards under Available or Drafts, then Move to claims: they wait in the Claim queue at their claim price, and keep their listing price for when they come back.

Checkout settings

Free mailing counts every card the buyer is having mailed in one delivery, at the price they paid, and the buyer's bill now says where they stand on it. Leave it empty to always charge the mailing fee.

Nobody's pay clock starts until the last released card is posted; everyone then gets the same minutes. Set the pace before releasing: Telegram throttles a group at roughly 20 messages a minute, so a big round with a short wait will be slowed down by Telegram, not by the bot.
